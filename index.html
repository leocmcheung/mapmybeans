How storage works

Your records live in beans.json. Edits are held in your browser first. To sync across devices, export the file and commit it to your GitHub repo — the site will read it on next load.

  1. Click Export JSON to download your current library.
  2. Replace beans.json at the root of your repo and commit.
  3. Others (and future-you) will load the updated data automatically.
